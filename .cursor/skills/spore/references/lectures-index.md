# Chris Hecker — Lectures Index

Source: [Category:Lectures](https://www.chrishecker.com/Category:Lectures) on chrishecker.com
(27 pages, confirmed via the wiki API). Hecker has spoken at GDC, SIGGRAPH, Eurographics and
others. Most pages provide slides (ppt) and mp3 audio; several note that the old slide-sync web
service died and videos are to be re-uploaded. Entries marked **[Spore]** are directly about
Spore or its systems; **[Core]** are the most useful for our evolution game.
